Founders come to Bubble for speed. They leave when the app becomes a fragile prototype. The difference is whether anyone designed auth, billing, AI, and ops as one system.

## What I ship

- **Bubble** front-end with role-based privacy rules
- **Supabase** or Bubble DB for data that needs SQL
- **OpenAI** features behind rate limits and audit logs
- **Stripe** subscriptions, trials, and metered usage
- **n8n** for webhooks, onboarding sequences, and admin alerts

Average time to beta on recent builds: **21 days** (see [Bubble SaaS Engine](/#lab) metrics).

## Bubble is the right call when

- You need UI iteration daily without deploy friction.
- Your team is non-technical but operator-strong.
- Integrations matter more than novel algorithms.

## Bubble is the wrong call when

- You need sub-50ms APIs at massive scale.
- Heavy real-time collaboration is the product.
- Regulated workloads need deep infra control from day one.
